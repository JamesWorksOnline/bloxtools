How much does Skibidi Toilet make?

At a base income of $350 million per second, one default Skibidi Toilet generates approximately $21 billion per minute, $1.26 trillion per hour and $30.24 trillion per day before additional mutations, traits or cash multipliers are applied.

Skibidi Toilet Mutations

Mutations can significantly increase Skibidi Toilet's income. A Gold Skibidi Toilet at 1.25× base income generates $437.5 million per second, while a Rainbow version at 10× generates $3.5 billion per second before additional trait bonuses.

Skibidi Toilet Payback Time

Using a $350 billion base cost and $350 million base income per second, the simple base-income payback time is about 1,000 seconds, or roughly 16 minutes and 40 seconds. This does not account for mutations, traits, cash multipliers or other bonuses.
