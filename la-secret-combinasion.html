How much does La Secret Combinasion make?

At a base income of $125 million per second, one default La Secret Combinasion generates approximately $7.5 billion per minute, $450 billion per hour and $10.8 trillion per day before mutations, traits or other income multipliers are applied.

How do you get La Secret Combinasion?

La Secret Combinasion is a Secret rarity Brainrot obtainable from the Secret Lucky Block. Community sources currently list its drop chance at approximately 0.5%, or 1 in 200 per block.
