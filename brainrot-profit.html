How does the profit calculator work?

The calculator divides the total purchase cost by total income per second to estimate break-even time. Profit is calculated by subtracting the purchase cost from the money earned during each time period. Actual in-game results can vary if your income changes because of mutations, traits, rebirths or other modifiers.
