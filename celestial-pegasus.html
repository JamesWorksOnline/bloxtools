How much does Celestial Pegasus make?

At a base income of $175 million per second, one default Celestial Pegasus generates approximately $10.5 billion per minute, $630 billion per hour and $15.12 trillion per day before mutations, traits or other cash multipliers are applied.

How do you get Celestial Pegasus?

Celestial Pegasus is a Secret rarity Brainrot associated with the Secret Lucky Block. Its listed base cost is $150 billion and its default income is $175 million per second.

Celestial Pegasus Mutations

Mutations can increase Celestial Pegasus income. A Gold Celestial Pegasus at 1.25× base income generates $218.75 million per second, while a Rainbow version at 10× generates $1.75 billion per second before additional trait bonuses.

Celestial Pegasus Payback Time

Using a $150 billion base cost and $175 million base income per second, the simple base-income payback time is about 857 seconds, or roughly 14 minutes and 17 seconds. Mutations, traits and other bonuses can change the effective earning rate.
