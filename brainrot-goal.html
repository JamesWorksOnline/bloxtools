How does the calculator work?

The calculator subtracts your current money from your target, then divides the remaining amount by your total income per second. Your total income is calculated as: Income per second × Quantity × Extra Multiplier .

Need your Brainrot income first?

If you don't know your exact income, use the BloxTools Brainrot Income Calculator first. You can calculate your Brainrot's income using its base income, mutation, traits, quantity and cash multiplier, then enter the final result here.

Example

If you're earning $600 million per second and starting from $0, reaching $100 trillion would take about 1 day, 22 hours and 18 minutes if your income remained constant.

Important

This is a mathematical estimate. Your actual time can differ if your income changes while playing, you spend money, gain additional Brainrots, change multipliers or the game updates its mechanics.
