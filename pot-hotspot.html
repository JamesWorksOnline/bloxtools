How to Get Pot Hotspot

Pot Hotspot is a Secret Brainrot associated with the Secret Lucky Block. It may also be obtained by stealing one from another player's base. Availability and game stats can change with updates.

Is Pot Hotspot Profitable?

Using a $600M base cost and $2.5M per-second base income, Pot Hotspot earns back the equivalent of its base cost in about 240 seconds, or 4 minutes.

Mutations, traits, quantity and cash multipliers can increase its earning power. Player-to-player trade value is separate from this income calculation.
