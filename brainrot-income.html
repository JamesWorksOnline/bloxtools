How the calculator works

Start with the Brainrot's normal base income. Select its mutation and any traits it currently has. BloxTools combines those bonuses and then applies your quantity and cash multiplier.

Income = Base Income × (Mutation + Trait Bonuses) × Quantity × Cash Multiplier

Steal a Brainrot changes over time, so the custom multiplier fields let you calculate newer mutations and traits that may not yet be listed here.
